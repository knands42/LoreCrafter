# LoreCrafter Product Overview

LoreCrafter is an AI-powered platform for RPG (Role-Playing Game) content creation. The platform assists players in generating rich, immersive content including characters, worlds, and campaigns with deep narrative structure and thematic consistency.

## Core Features

- **AI-Generated Characters**: Create characters with backstories, personalities, and visual descriptions
- **World Building**: Generate worlds with history, timelines, and cultural context
- **Campaign Creation**: Build campaigns with narrative setups, NPCs, and plot elements
- **Content Linking**: Connect characters to worlds and campaigns for coherent storytelling
- **PDF Export**: Export character sheets and content for tabletop sessions
- **Community Sharing**: Share creations publicly for community reuse and inspiration

## Target Users

- RPG players and Dungeon Masters
- Tabletop gaming enthusiasts
- Creative writers and worldbuilders
- Gaming communities seeking collaborative content creation

## Technical Focus

The platform emphasizes narrative consistency, AI-driven content generation, and seamless integration between different content types to support long-term storytelling campaigns.
